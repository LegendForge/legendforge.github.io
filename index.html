Selected work

See what the team's actually built.

Real portfolio pieces and client reviews from the team's past work — pulled straight from our own track record, not staged examples.

Twitch
Problem
A 407-follower variety and fighting-game streamer had strong branding and real on-stream personality, but streamed inconsistently across five games with multi-week gaps between sessions. Clips never left Twitch, titles had no search hook, and thumbnails were raw screenshots. Overall growth score: 41/100.
What we did
Locked in a 3-streams-a-week schedule for 8 weeks, built a short-form clipping pipeline (3–5 clips/week to TikTok/Shorts/Reels with a CTA back to Twitch), introduced a title formula (game + hook + character/mode), standardized thumbnails around one template, and made fighting games the flagship content pillar with a recurring "Anime Debate of the Day" segment.
Result
Followers grew from 407 to 4.2k and live viewers from 1 to 78, as shown below.

Before / after comparison of the channel's Twitch profile.

Twitch
Problem
A 572-follower variety and IRL streamer already had the hard part solved — a fixed twice-daily schedule, lively chat, and a working Discord. What held the channel back was presentation: no visual identity, a bio that read like a timetable, and clippable moments that never left Twitch. Overall growth score: 54/100.
What we did
Built a weekly clip-to-Shorts pipeline from the existing Clips tab, created one consistent thumbnail template with a real accent color and name badge, replaced repetitive milestone-countdown titles with per-stream hooks, filled out the About tab to match the Home page, and gave each game a loose weekly slot instead of random rotation.
Result
Followers grew from 625 to 6.2k and live viewers from 5 to 154, as shown below.

Before / after comparison of the channel's Twitch profile.

Twitch
Problem
A 1.5K-follower variety and co-op streamer had a distinctive mascot brand but nothing around it supporting growth — an empty About tab, a bio reading "I play when I have time," six games with no stated niche, and titles with no search intent. Overall growth score: 32/100.
What we did
Rewrote the bio to state niche, schedule and personality in one line, moved it above the ad banners in the About tab, replaced placeholder social links with real handles, narrowed content to two pillars (co-op chaos and cozy survival builds), switched to search-friendly titles, and started clipping 1-2 moments per stream to short-form with consistent mascot branding.
Result
The channel reached 4.8K followers, as shown in the before/after comparison below.
